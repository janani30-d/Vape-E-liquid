A CLOSER LOOK

Understanding Chemical Composition

E-liquids may contain several chemical substances. Their composition can vary, and the presence of particular ingredients does not establish that a product is safe.

Understanding ingredient terminology is an important part of learning about potential exposure and health risks.

01

Nicotine

An addictive substance that can affect the nervous and cardiovascular systems.

02

Propylene Glycol (PG)

A chemical compound used in various industrial and consumer applications.

03

Vegetable Glycerin (VG)

A substance used in pharmaceutical, cosmetic, and other formulations.

04

Flavouring Chemicals

Chemical compounds used to create different flavour profiles. Inhalation safety cannot be assumed from food use.

HEALTH & SAFETY AWARENESS

Ingredient Information Does Not Mean Safety

Nicotine exposure, chemical inhalation, and other potential health effects are important considerations. This information is educational only and does not recommend or promote the use of electronic cigarettes.

COMPONENT AWARENESS

Understanding Accessory Components

Learn about common electronic and material components associated with electronic cigarette devices, including their general characteristics and potential safety concerns.

01

Charging Components

Electronic charging components are associated with transferr electrical energy to recharge battery systems. Their condition and compatibility are electrical safety consider.

Damaged cables, connectors, or batteries can create electrical and overheating hazards.
02

Electrical Components

Electronic systems may contain circuit boards, connectors, wiring, and protective components. These elements are part of the device's general electrical architecture.

Exposed wiring and damaged electronic parts may present electrical safety risks.
03

Material Components

Electronic devices can contain metals, plastics, glass, insulating materials, and other manufactured components. Material quality and condition are relevant to general product safety.

Damaged or deteriorating materials may expose internal components or create additional hazards.
SAFETY AWARENESS

Understand Electrical and Material Risks

Electronic components and batteries can present electrical, overheating, fire, and disposal hazards. Damaged electronic equipment should not be handled or modified. Follow applicable safety and disposal regulations.
