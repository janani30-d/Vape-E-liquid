HEALTH & PUBLIC SAFETY

Awareness Is Everyone's Responsibility

Electronic cigarettes and nicotine products raise important health and safety concerns. We believe these risks should be communicated clearly, alongside relevant legal restrictions and public health guidance.

Public health, safety, and legal awareness remain essential.
01

Age Protection

Age restrictions are an important part of public health protection. Electronic cigarette products are subject to legal restrictions in India.

02

Health Considerations

Nicotine is addictive, and vaping can expose people to substances that may harm their health. These risks should never be overlooked.

03

Legal Responsibility
