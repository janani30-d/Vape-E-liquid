LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Understanding the Legal Landscape

Learn about India's legal framework concerning electronic cigarettes, the restrictions established by law, and the importance of public awareness.

ACT NO. 42 OF 2019

The Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act, 2019

The Government of India enacted the Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act, 2019, to prohibit specified activities involving electronic cigarettes in the interest of public health.

The Act covers electronic cigarettes and associated activities, including production, manufacture, import, export, transport, sale, distribution, storage and advertisement.

Legal Awareness Matters Understanding applicable laws helps promote responsible public awareness.
INDIAN LEGISLATION Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act, 2019
IMPORTANT PROVISIONS

Key Areas Covered by the Act

The legislation establishes prohibitions across the following areas.

01

Production & Manufacture

The Act prohibits the production and manufacture of electronic cigarettes, including their parts for components.

02

Import & Export

Import and export of electronic cigarettes are among the activities prohibited under the Act including age.

03

Transport, Sale

The legislation prohibits the transport, sale and distribution of electronic cigarettes, complete products or parts.

04

Storage & Advertisement

The Act also addresses storage and prohibits advertis that directly or indirectly promote electronic cigarette.

IMPORTANT LEGAL NOTICE

Understand the Legal Responsibilities

The Act provides penalties for violations of its provisions. This information is intended for public awareness and is not a substitute for professional legal advice.

HEALTH & PUBLIC AWARENESS

Understand the Health Concerns

Electronic cigarettes can expose users to nicotine and other potentially harmful substances. Understanding these health concerns is an important part of making informed public-health decisions.

HEALTH INFORMATION

E-cigarette Aerosol Is Not Simply Water Vapour

E-cigarettes heat a liquid to create an aerosol that users inhale. This aerosol may contain nicotine, flavouring chemicals, fine particles and other substances that can affect health.
